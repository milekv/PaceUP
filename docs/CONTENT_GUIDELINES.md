# Content Guidelines

PaceUP copy should feel active, clear, and supportive.

## Voice

- direct,
- optimistic,
- not overly competitive,
- friendly without being childish,
- specific when showing progress.

## Examples

- Prefer "Start today's workout" over "Click here".
- Prefer "You earned 80 XP" over "Success".
- Prefer "No activities yet" over "No data".
- Prefer "Try again" over technical error text.
