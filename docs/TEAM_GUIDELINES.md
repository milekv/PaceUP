# Team Guidelines

Teams should make PaceUP more motivating without making solo progress feel secondary.

## Team Basics

- A team has a name, goal, activity focus, and member list.
- Team progress should summarize shared consistency, not shame inactive users.
- Joining and leaving a team should be simple.
- Team cards should show enough context before the user joins.

## Healthy Social Design

- Reward encouragement and shared activity.
- Avoid public leaderboards that punish beginners.
- Keep private activity details out of public team previews.
- Make moderation and reporting possible before teams become public.

## Useful Metrics

- weekly team activity count,
- active members,
- shared quest progress,
- upcoming event preparation,
- recent completed group activities.
