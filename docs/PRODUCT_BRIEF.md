# Product Brief

## Product

PaceUP is a running pace and training planning app.

## Target Users

- Beginner runners preparing for their first organized race.
- Recreational runners improving 5K, 10K, or half marathon times.
- Runners who want a simple plan without a complex coaching platform.

## Problem

Runners often track activities, but still struggle to connect goals with the correct training paces and weekly structure.

## Solution

PaceUP provides:

- Pace calculation.
- Goal planning.
- Weekly training structure.
- Progress summaries.
- Clear next actions.

## MVP Scope

- Static dashboard.
- Pace calculator logic.
- Goal presets.
- Simple training week planner.
- GitHub Pages deployment.

## Out of Scope for MVP

- User accounts.
- Paid coaching.
- Wearable integrations.
- Social features.
- Long-term data storage.

## Success Signals

- A visitor understands the product in under 10 seconds.
- A runner can calculate goal pace in under 30 seconds.
- The repo is easy to run, review, and extend.
