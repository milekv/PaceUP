# Accessibility Checklist

PaceUP should feel fast and usable for people training outdoors, on mobile screens, and in imperfect lighting.

## Navigation

- Primary actions are reachable by keyboard.
- Focus states are visible on interactive elements.
- Back and close actions are predictable.
- Tabs, segmented controls, and menus expose clear selected states.

## Content

- Buttons use action-oriented labels.
- Form errors explain how to recover.
- Empty states show the next useful action.
- Numbers such as pace, XP, and volume include enough context.

## Visual Design

- Text has enough contrast against backgrounds.
- Important states are not communicated by color alone.
- Tap targets are large enough for mobile use.
- Motion does not block task completion.

## Product Flows

- Starting and finishing an activity remains clear with one hand.
- Quest and badge notifications do not cover critical controls.
- Profile and settings screens remain usable with partial data.
- Loading states do not look like broken content.
