# Code of Conduct

PaceUP aims to stay practical, respectful, and learning-friendly.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear and constructive.
- Focus feedback on the work.
- Respect different experience levels.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or insults.
- Publishing private information.
- Spam or off-topic promotion.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block participants when needed.
